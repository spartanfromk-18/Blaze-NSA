# 30-second demo script

- **0–04s:** Open Blaze Field Advisor. Subtitle: “A Hindi-first farm comparison using NASA climate data.”
- **04–09s:** Tap Start. Agra loads with live NASA POWER temperature and rainfall badges.
- **09–16s:** Toggle “Save water” and show the visible 1.5 weight and reordered cards.
- **16–23s:** Open the top rotation. Show water, soil, climate-fit, income, evidence, gaps, and risks.
- **23–30s:** Tap “सुनिए”. Hindi voice plays while the transcript remains visible. Subtitle: “This is advice, not an order—ask a local agricultural expert.”
