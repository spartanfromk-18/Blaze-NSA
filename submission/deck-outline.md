# Blaze Field Advisor — 7-slide outline

1. **Title:** Blaze Field Advisor — NASA climate data for better farm choices.
2. **Problem:** Shifting monsoon, water-heavy rice-wheat cycles, and text-heavy English advisories leave smallholders without a clear comparison.
3. **NASA data:** Live NASA POWER climatology for Agra; temperature and precipitation normals are fetched in the browser and visibly badged.
4. **Comparison:** Four rotation cards compare water, soil, climate fit, and curated income indices.
5. **Recommendation:** Farmer priorities re-rank the options; Hindi voice reads the selected recommendation.
6. **Honesty and methods:** Rule-based, not ML. Curated approximations need agronomist review; missing soil, irrigation, pest, and market data are explicit.
7. **Next steps:** Add GPM IMERG, SMAP, MODIS NDVI, and GRACE evidence after validation.
